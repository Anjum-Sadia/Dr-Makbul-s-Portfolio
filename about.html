About Me

My Journey, Expertise, and Commitment to Pediatric Care

Dr. Md Makbul Hossain

MBBS, MD (Pediatrics), FCPS (Pediatric Cardiology)

With over 15 years of dedicated experience in pediatric medicine, my mission is to provide the highest standard of care for children's heart health. I believe in a holistic approach that combines state-of-the-art medical technology with compassionate, family-centered communication.

My journey has been driven by a passion for helping the most vulnerable patients, ensuring every child has the opportunity for a healthy and fulfilling life. I am committed to continuous learning and applying the latest advancements in pediatric cardiology.

  • Expertise in Echocardiography: Utilizing advanced imaging to accurately diagnose complex heart conditions.
  • NICU Specialist: Proficient in critical care for newborns with congenital heart defects.
  • Long-Term Management: Focused on comprehensive care plans for chronic pediatric heart conditions.

My Approach to Care

My practice is built on three core principles to ensure the best outcomes for my patients and their families.

Patient-Centered Care

Every child is unique. I tailor treatment plans to the specific needs of the patient, ensuring their comfort and well-being are always the top priority.

Advanced Technology

Leveraging the latest diagnostic tools and medical technologies allows for more accurate diagnoses and effective, minimally invasive treatments.

Family Partnership

I believe in working closely with parents and guardians, keeping them informed and involved in every step of the decision-making process.
